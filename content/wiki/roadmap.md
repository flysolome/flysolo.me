FlySolo currently focuses on the connected commercial workflow from opportunity to proposal, pricing, contract, signature, invoice handoff, and payment context. Otto can also explain a contract or clause you received in plain language.

The capabilities below are planned. Phase labels communicate sequence, not delivery dates.

## Phase 2: Reusable professional context

- Professional profiles
- Structured opportunity intake
- Trust Center

## Phase 3: Contract intelligence

- Full AI contract review
- Contract risk detection
- Negotiation assistant

These capabilities build on Otto's current plain-language contract explanation. They are broader review and decision-support workflows, not a claim of legal advice.

## Phase 4: Reputation and business insight

- Reputation layer
- Verified engagements and professional verification
- Business insights
- Expanded language and regional support

## Phase 5: Proactive business guidance

- AI business advisor
- Forecasting
- Advanced ecosystem of connected tools

Roadmap scope can change as FlySolo learns from real freelancer workflows. Planned items are not included in the current product promise until they ship.
