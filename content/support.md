For product help, account questions, or assistance connecting FlySolo to an
MCP client, email [hello@flysolo.me](mailto:hello@flysolo.me).

Please include the approximate time of the issue and the FlySolo feature you
were using. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, client secrets, or confidential
customer documents by email.

The FlySolo MCP connection requires an eligible FlySolo account. The initial
release is read-only: it can retrieve workspace data and explain document
sections, but it cannot purchase subscriptions, process billing, sign
documents, delete records, or perform administrative actions.
