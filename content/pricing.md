## Try the workflow before billing starts

Use FlySolo for one limited month with a recent or anonymised client opportunity. Your paid period starts after that month, so you can judge the product on work you would genuinely use.

| Plan | Price after trial | Best fit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| **Starter** | **€19/month** | Organising opportunities and preparing the first commercial decisions |
| **Professional** | **€39/month** | Running proposals, pricing, scope, agreements, and follow-up as one workflow |
| **Business** | **€79/month** | A broader operating setup with more advanced workflow and business support |

## Starter

For freelancers who want a dependable place to qualify opportunities and stop rebuilding context from inbox threads.

- Opportunity and client records
- Qualification support and focused follow-up questions
- Core proposal workflow

## Professional

For senior freelancers who regularly handle proposals, rate discussions, scope boundaries, agreements, and client approvals. This is the plan closest to the complete FlySolo deal workflow.

- Everything in Starter
- Pricing and scope decision support
- Agreement, approval, and signature workflow
- Moneybird handoff and payment context

## Business

For freelancers whose commercial workflow needs more capacity, deeper support, or a broader operating setup as FlySolo expands.

- Everything in Professional
- Higher workflow capacity
- Advanced support and future business workflows

## Before you choose

**Is FlySolo accounting software?**  
No. FlySolo manages the commercial workflow around an opportunity. Moneybird remains the accounting handoff.

**Will Otto send something without approval?**  
No. Client-facing work waits for your review.

**What should I bring to the trial?**  
A recruiter message, client brief, rate question, or anonymised opportunity that is current enough to be useful.
