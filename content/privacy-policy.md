FlySolo uses privacy-conscious defaults. The Google tag loads with analytics, advertising, and personalisation storage denied; analytics storage is enabled only after you accept analytics cookies.

## Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate website usage, such as page views, device type, and referral sources. Google Consent Mode denies analytics and advertising storage by default. Google signals and advertising personalisation are disabled in the site configuration.

## Cookies

We use tarteaucitron to manage cookie consent and remember that choice. A first-party `flysolo_lang` preference remembers a language you explicitly select. These necessary preferences are separate from optional analytics storage.

You can accept, deny, or change analytics preferences from the cookie settings control in the footer or the cookie icon.

## Trial applications

When you request a trial, FlySolo uses the contact, business, and workflow details you submit to review your application and contact you about access. Do not include confidential client information in the form.

## MCP connection

When you connect FlySolo to an MCP client such as ChatGPT, the client receives
only the workspace data returned by the tools you explicitly invoke. FlySolo
uses OAuth to identify your account and verify workspace membership. The MCP
gateway does not expose internal service credentials, and the initial release
does not provide billing, purchasing, signing, deletion, or administrative
actions. Requests and audit records follow the retention and security practices
described in the FlySolo product and security documentation.

## Contact

If you email FlySolo, we use your message and contact details only to respond to your request.

## Your choices and requests

You can reopen cookie settings at any time. To ask about, correct, or request deletion of trial application or contact information, email [hello@flysolo.me](mailto:hello@flysolo.me).
