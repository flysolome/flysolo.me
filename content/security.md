## You remain the decision maker

Otto can analyse context, prepare a draft, and suggest a next action. A proposal, agreement, signature request, or invoice action does not become a client commitment until you review and approve it.

This boundary is part of the product model, not a sentence added to an AI disclaimer.

## Approval before client-facing actions

- Review the content Otto prepared
- See which commercial assumptions shaped it
- Edit the draft or return to the underlying decision
- Approve the client-facing action explicitly

## Traceable commercial decisions

A rate, scope boundary, or contract term should not disappear into a chat transcript. FlySolo keeps the decision attached to the opportunity so you can understand what changed, what was accepted, and what the next document is based on.

## European data handling

FlySolo is designed around EU-region infrastructure and European privacy expectations. Product scope, retention, access controls, and integrations are treated as operational design decisions rather than left to individual prompts.

## Integrations keep their boundaries

Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 remain communication and document tools. Moneybird remains the accounting destination. FlySolo coordinates the commercial workflow between them without claiming to replace their specialist responsibilities.

## AI scope

Otto supports commercial preparation and workflow decisions, including plain-language explanations of contracts you receive. Those explanations are informational: FlySolo does not position Otto as an accountant, tax adviser, payroll provider, or lawyer. Professional advice remains appropriate where the decision requires it.
